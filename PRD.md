## 1. Website Overview

### What is the TNT Capital Website?

The TNT Capital website is the public-facing digital platform for TNT Capital. It is designed to present TNT as a long-term investment institution and venture builder focused on disciplined capital allocation, original thinking, entrepreneurship, and Vietnam's long-term development.

The website should help visitors understand:

- What TNT Capital does
- How TNT Capital thinks
- What TNT Capital invests in
- What TNT Capital builds
- Who is behind the institution
- How to contact or work with TNT

The website is not only an introduction to the firm. It is also a publishing platform for research, investment thinking, market analysis, institutional principles, and long-term ideas.

---

### The Core Problem

TNT Capital is newly established and needs a credible public presence that clearly communicates its identity and activity.

Without a clear website:

- Visitors may not understand what TNT Capital does
- The relationship between investing, research, and venture building may feel unclear
- Research and institutional thinking may remain scattered across different platforms
- Strategic partners and founders may not know how to approach TNT
- The institution may appear less established or less focused than it actually is
- The public may confuse TNT Capital with a personal investment project rather than a serious long-term institution

---

### The Solution

The website will bring TNT Capital's major activities into one structured platform. The main areas are:

- Asset Management
- Thinking
- Ventures
- Team

The website will explain TNT's investment activity without disclosing private fund information, confidential holdings, investor information, or internal performance. It will also provide a publishing system for research and articles in both **English** and **Vietnamese**.

---

### Website Objectives

The website should:

- Establish TNT Capital's institutional credibility
- Clearly explain TNT's investment and venture-building activities
- Publish high-quality research and long-term thinking
- Present selected investments and ventures
- Attract strategic partners and long-term investors
- Provide founders and operators with a clear way to contact TNT
- Build a consistent public record of TNT's thinking and development
- Present Vietnam as an important part of TNT's global perspective

---

### Website Philosophy

The website should communicate **substance before scale**.

TNT Capital should not try to appear larger than it currently is. It should appear focused, serious, disciplined, and transparent about what it does. The website should only present activities, people, ventures, investments, and capabilities that genuinely exist.

> The goal is not to create the appearance of a large financial institution.  
> The goal is to create a credible foundation that can grow with the institution over time.

## 2. Visitor Experience

### First Impression

When a visitor enters the website, they should immediately understand that TNT Capital is:

- A long-term investment institution
- A venture builder
- Globally oriented
- Deeply interested in Vietnam's development
- Driven by discipline, integrity, research, and long-term thinking

The first impression should feel **calm, structured, modern, and credible**.

The website should not feel like:

- A personal blog
- A financial trading platform
- A technology startup landing page
- A luxury investment firm
- A large institution pretending to have capabilities it does not yet have

---

### Homepage Experience

The homepage should introduce TNT Capital at an institutional level. Visitors should be able to understand the firm through four main areas:

1. Asset Management
2. Thinking
3. Ventures
4. Team

The homepage should also include:

- A clear institutional introduction
- TNT's global perspective and Vietnam conviction
- Featured research or articles
- Selected investments or ventures
- A brief introduction to the team
- A clear contact option

> The About content should be integrated into the homepage and Team section rather than existing as a separate top-level page.

---

### Asset Management Experience

Visitors entering Asset Management should understand:

- How TNT approaches investing
- What types of opportunities TNT studies
- What principles guide investment decisions
- How TNT evaluates value, risk, quality, and long-term potential
- The difference between TNT's public-market and private-market activities

The page should describe real investment activity without mentioning the private fund directly.

**The website should not disclose:**

- Fund size
- Investor identities
- Detailed fund structure
- Private performance
- Confidential holdings
- Internal reporting
- Subscription or fundraising information

---

### Thinking Experience

Thinking should function as TNT Capital's publishing platform. Visitors should be able to explore:

- Research
- Investment memos
- Market commentary
- Essays
- Founder's notes
- Vietnam 2045
- TNT Capital Doctrine

The publishing system should feel deliberate and selective. TNT is not a media company and does not need to publish daily. Each publication should reflect meaningful research, institutional thinking, or a clear point of view.

> **Vietnam 2045** and the **TNT Capital Doctrine** should be treated as cornerstone publications rather than ordinary articles.

---

### Ventures Experience

Visitors entering Ventures should understand:

- What companies or projects TNT has built
- What companies TNT supports
- How TNT approaches venture creation
- What role TNT plays beyond providing capital
- How founders and operators can approach TNT

Only active or publicly presentable ventures should appear. Unlaunched concepts, internal ideas, and unfinished projects should remain private.

---

### Team Experience

The Team section should present TNT as founder-led while keeping the institution as the central identity. The founder should be visible, but the website should not feel centered around a personal brand.

The Team section may include:

- Institutional introduction
- Founder profile
- Current team members
- Advisors
- Values and working principles
- Opportunities to work with TNT when relevant

Empty positions, fictional departments, or undeveloped team structures should not be displayed.

---

### Contact Experience

Visitors should have one clear contact path. The contact system should allow visitors to select the reason for contacting TNT, including:

- Strategic partnership
- Investment opportunity
- Founder or company introduction
- Research inquiry
- Media inquiry
- General inquiry

The contact process should feel professional and simple.

## 3. Target Audiences

### Primary Audience

#### Strategic Partners

Strategic partners are the highest-priority audience. These may include:

- Institutions
- Businesses
- Universities
- Research organizations
- Investors
- Operators
- Organizations involved in Vietnam's development
- International partners interested in Southeast Asia

**Their main needs are:**

- Understanding TNT's credibility
- Understanding TNT's long-term direction
- Reviewing TNT's thinking and institutional principles
- Identifying areas for collaboration
- Contacting TNT directly

---

### Secondary Audience

#### Long-Term Investors

Long-term investors may want to understand:

- TNT's investment philosophy
- Its approach to risk
- Its decision-making principles
- Its view of Vietnam and global markets
- Its research quality
- Its investment discipline

The website should build trust without disclosing private information.

#### Readers and Researchers

Readers and researchers may include:

- Students
- Academics
- Market professionals
- Investors
- Entrepreneurs
- People interested in Vietnam
- People interested in long-term business and economic development

They should be able to:

- Read research
- Browse articles by topic
- Access English and Vietnamese versions
- Understand the context and publication date of each piece
- Explore related articles

#### Founders and Operators

Founders and operators should be able to understand:

- What TNT looks for
- What types of companies or people TNT supports
- How TNT thinks about innovation and long-term value
- How to introduce a company or opportunity
- How TNT may support ventures beyond capital

---

### Lower-Priority Audience

#### General Public

The website should remain understandable to the general public, but it should not be designed primarily for mass attention. The language should be clear and accessible without oversimplifying the institution's thinking.

### Main Visitor Flows

#### Flow 1 — Understanding TNT Capital

1. Visitor enters the homepage
2. Visitor reads the institutional introduction
3. Visitor reviews the four main areas
4. Visitor opens Asset Management, Thinking, Ventures, or Team
5. Visitor develops a clear understanding of TNT's identity and activities

#### Flow 2 — Reading Research

1. Visitor opens Thinking
2. Visitor browses publications by category
3. Visitor opens an article
4. Visitor reads the publication
5. Visitor explores related research
6. Visitor may switch between English and Vietnamese where both versions exist

#### Flow 3 — Reviewing Investment Activity

1. Visitor opens Asset Management
2. Visitor reads TNT's investment philosophy and approach
3. Visitor reviews public-market or private-market activity
4. Visitor views selected investments when available
5. Visitor may contact TNT for a relevant inquiry

#### Flow 4 — Exploring Ventures

1. Visitor opens Ventures
2. Visitor reviews TNT's venture-building approach
3. Visitor views active ventures or portfolio companies
4. Visitor opens an individual venture page
5. Visitor may contact TNT with a company or partnership proposal

#### Flow 5 — Contacting TNT

1. Visitor opens Contact
2. Visitor selects the inquiry type
3. Visitor enters contact information and a message
4. Visitor may include a company, organization, or relevant website
5. Visitor submits the inquiry
6. TNT receives the inquiry in an organized format

#### Flow 6 — Switching Language

1. Visitor selects English or Vietnamese
2. Visitor remains on the equivalent page when a translated version exists
3. If no translated version exists, the visitor is directed to the closest available language page
4. The selected language remains consistent as the visitor continues browsing

## 6. Design and Technical Requirements

### Visual Direction

The website should feel:

- Minimal
- Modern
- Structured
- Calm
- Credible
- Institutional
- Thoughtful

The dominant visual tone should use **off-white backgrounds**. The final color system may continue evolving, but the website should use:

- Restrained colors
- Strong contrast
- Clear hierarchy
- Generous spacing
- Minimal decoration
- Purposeful imagery

**The website should avoid:**

- Luxury-coded design
- Gold-heavy visual language
- Excessive animation
- Technology-startup aesthetics
- Loud gradients
- Visual clutter
- Generic corporate stock photography
- Dense financial dashboards on the public site

---

### Typography

Typography should be:

- Clear
- Modern
- Highly readable
- Suitable for long research articles
- Consistent across English and Vietnamese

> Vietnamese characters must display correctly across all supported fonts and devices.

---

### Responsive Design

The website must work well on:

| Device | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Desktop | Full layout with all content and navigation |
| Laptop | Full layout, may adjust spacing |
| Tablet | Adapted layout, all content accessible |
| Mobile | Streamlined layout, no important content removed |

---

### Performance

- Pages should load quickly
- Images should be optimized
- Long articles should remain easy to navigate
- Mobile performance should remain strong
- Language switching should not create unnecessary delays

---

### Accessibility

- Text should have sufficient contrast
- Navigation should work with a keyboard
- Images should support descriptive text
- Headings should follow a clear hierarchy
- Forms should include understandable labels and error messages
- The website should remain usable when motion is reduced

---

### Security

- Contact forms should include spam protection
- Administrator access should be secure
- Uploaded documents should be managed safely
- The content system should support backups
- Private investment information should never be exposed through public pages
- The website should collect only necessary visitor information

---

### Search Visibility

The website should support:

- Search-engine-readable pages
- Clear page titles
- Page descriptions
- Article metadata
- Publication dates
- English and Vietnamese page indexing
- Shareable article previews
- Clean page links

---

### Bilingual Requirements

English and Vietnamese should operate as two complete language versions of the same website.

**URL structure:**
```
tntcapital.com/en/   ← English
tntcapital.com/vi/   ← Vietnamese
```

- Each language version should be written and reviewed independently
- Vietnamese content should not rely on automatic translation
- When an article exists in only one language, the website should clearly indicate that the alternate version is not yet available

---

### Reliability

- Published content should not disappear during updates
- Draft content should save automatically where possible
- Contact submissions should be stored or delivered reliably
- Broken links should be monitored
- The website should support regular backups

## 7. Website Positioning and Boundaries

### Positioning

TNT Capital is a long-term investment institution and venture builder that allocates capital, develops original thinking, and supports companies and individuals capable of building lasting value.

The website should position TNT through four connected activities:

1. **Investing**
2. **Research and thinking**
3. **Venture building**
4. **Long-term partnerships**

TNT should appear globally oriented while maintaining a strong conviction in Vietnam's long-term development. The institution should be presented as founder-led, but the founder should remain secondary to the TNT Capital identity.

---

### What the Website Should Communicate

The website should communicate that TNT:

- Actively studies and participates in investment opportunities
- Thinks in long time horizons
- Values discipline, integrity, competence, and steady execution
- Invests across public and private markets
- Builds and supports ventures
- Publishes original research and institutional thinking
- Views Vietnam as an important long-term strategic opportunity
- Is open to credible strategic partnerships and investment opportunities

---

### What the Website Should Not Imply

The website should not imply that TNT:

- Is larger than it is
- Has a large investment team when it does not
- Manages public funds
- Accepts capital from the public
- Has public performance figures when it does not
- Has portfolio companies that are not formally connected to TNT
- Has active offices, teams, or capabilities that do not yet exist
- Publishes with the frequency of a media company
- Supports every type of founder or investment opportunity

---

### Institutional Boundary

The website should distinguish between:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Formal investment activity** | TNT Capital's managed investment operations |
| **Active ventures** | Companies or projects TNT has built or formally supports |
| **Published thinking** | Institutional research, analysis, and frameworks |
| **Founder's personal opinion** | Personal voice, expressed only in Founder's Notes |

> Founder's Notes may contain a more personal voice, but all other institutional publications should follow TNT Capital's structured and analytical tone.

---

### Long-Term Role of the Website

The website should grow alongside TNT Capital.

**At launch**, it should:
- Establish credibility
- Organize research
- Explain investment activity
- Present ventures
- Create a clear path for contact

**As TNT develops**, the website may expand to include:
- More public portfolio information
- More team members
- Deeper venture case studies
- Investor access
- Private reporting
- Events and presentations
- Expanded research libraries

> The website should be designed as a **durable institutional platform** rather than a temporary landing page.

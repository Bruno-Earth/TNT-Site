10 / Scroll choreography

The transitions are quieter than the homepage, and they still hand one object forward.

TransitionVisual shiftStory meaning
Arrival holdsThe editorial rule is already a line on White. The statement reveals by whitespace, not by a generic fade.The world has already become structure. The page begins there.
Arrival → WorkThe single rule extends, then divides into four measured segments over the four activities.Identity becomes the work of the firm.
Work → PrinciplesThe four segments lose equal spread. Spacing tightens. Four short rules restack into the 01–04 principle column. Mist resolves to Soft Gray.Activity becomes character. Noise drops.
Principles → FounderThe principle column releases. One remaining rule moves to the top of the founder block and sets the width of the biography, not the portrait.Character acquires a name. The person sits inside the structure.
Founder → PeopleIf people exist, the rule opens into a short row of real names. If not, the rule travels on toward the close with no empty interval.The institution is only as peopled as it is.
CloseWhite releases into Dark Green. A low horizon returns. Motion slows until only Contact is interactive.Identity becomes an invitation.
FooterDark field releases into a static utility footer.The page resolves into the institution.

Pace. Do not pin Arrival, The work, or Principles. The visitor should be able to read each chapter without fighting a scrub. If a handoff makes type move under the eye, drop the transform and keep the incoming rule in the right place.

DivideThe lengthened rule splits into four segments of equal length. They settle above Investing, Insights, Venture, and Partnerships.

Homepage continuity. On the homepage, one rule fractures into six research windows: one worldview, many questions. On About, one rule divides into four activities: one firm, the work it does. Do not reuse the six-window motif here. The counts mean different things.

18 / Content boundaries

The layout is not allowed to imply a firm that the content does not support.

Publish

  • Who TNT is, in institutional language
  • The four activities, without performance or fund detail
  • Short principles, with Manifesto as the long form
  • A real founder name, role, and approved biography
  • Team and advisors who are public and current
  • One contact path
  • English and Vietnamese as separate writing, not a mirrored translation

Do not publish

  • Fund size, structure, investors, or private performance
  • Holdings and internal reporting
  • Offices, departments, or headcount that do not exist
  • Empty team categories or fictional advisors
  • A careers grid with no approved role
  • Personal-brand framing that puts the founder ahead of TNT
  • Automatic Vietnamese pasted under English sentences
